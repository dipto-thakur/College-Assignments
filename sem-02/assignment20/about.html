Our History

Ashford Institute of Technology was founded in 1978 by a group of five engineers who wanted a college that stayed small on purpose. That's mostly still true, we've grown from 120 students to 1,800, but batch sizes in every core class are capped well below what most institutes allow.

We added our computer science program in 1994 and our electronics program in 2003, and both have since become the two most applied to programs on campus.

What We Stand For

  • Small class sizes, capped at 40 students for every core engineering course
  • Faculty who teach, not just senior researchers who show up occasionally
  • Labs open to undergraduates from year one, not reserved for postgrad research
  • A placement office that works with employers year round, not just in March
