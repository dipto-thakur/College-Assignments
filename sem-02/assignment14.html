What was wrong, and what changed

  • The type had no hierarchy or restraint, a decorative font at 40px next to a dense wall of 13px body text with no breathing room. The redesign uses one serif for headings and a plain system sans for body copy, with real spacing between them.
  • Navy, red, and yellow together fought for attention and had nothing to do with a bakery. The redesign pulls its palette from the product itself, cream, wheat, and a warm brown, so the color scheme actually reads as "bakery."
  • The layout depended on a floated image and no real structure, which breaks on smaller screens. The redesign uses a proper grid for the menu highlights so it reflows cleanly instead of collapsing.
  • The copy was one long paragraph doing five jobs at once, plus a shouty call to action. The redesign gives the hero one short promise, a single clear button, and moves supporting details into their own cards.
