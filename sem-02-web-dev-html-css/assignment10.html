Meera Nair

I make objects out of ink and pressure — cards, posters and books, set and printed by hand in a small studio in Pondicherry.

About

I started as a typesetter's apprentice ten years ago and never really left the press room. My work sits somewhere between graphic design and craft — every piece is planned on a screen but finished by hand, one impression at a time. I care about the weight of paper, the bite of a letterpress plate, and giving old techniques new use.
