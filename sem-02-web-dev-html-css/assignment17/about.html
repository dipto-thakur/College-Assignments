Our Story

Chapter & Verse started as a single shelf inside a coffee shop in 2012. The owner, Leela, kept bringing in books she'd already read and telling customers to take them if they promised to bring one back. Three years later that shelf became its own storefront two doors down.

We're still small on purpose. No more than a few thousand titles at any time, chosen by people who work here, not by a sales algorithm.

What We Care About

  • Books chosen by staff who've actually read them, not bestseller lists
  • A store that's quiet enough to read a first chapter standing at the shelf
  • Supporting local writers with a dedicated section near the front
  • Keeping prices honest, secondhand books priced to move, not to sit
